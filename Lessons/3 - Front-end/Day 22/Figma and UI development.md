### Repository


Figma is a collaborative interface-design tool. It lets us explore a page's structure, spacing, reusable parts and interactions before writing the interface in code.

Today the lesson is taught from live, editable Figma frames. Work through them in order: read the prompt, change the example, then use its checklist to verify what changed. The frames cover layout, responsive resizing, components, variants, interactions and a small Motion demonstration.
